DESCRIPTION

The Petro Station Management System is a simple system that helps the station manage fuel types, pumps, staff, shifts, fuel sales, stock levels and restocking.

SYSTEM USERS

  • Manager
  • Attendants
  • Supervisors
  • Cashier

SYSTEM ACTIVITIES

  • Staff and customer registration
  • Fuel type and pump registration
  • Fuel sales transactions
  • Shift allocation
  • Daily stock records and restocking

SYSTEM TABLES

No. Table Purpose
1 fuel Stores fuel types, prices and current stock
2 staff Stores staff information
3 pump Stores pump details and the fuel each one dispences
4 shift_allocation Stores which staff work on which shift and pump
5 transactions Stores fuel sales
6 stock_details stores daily opening and closing stock
7 stocking stores fuel deliveries and cost prices
